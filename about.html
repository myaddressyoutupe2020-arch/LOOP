About LOOP

A community-first company built to bridge the gap between learning and real-world application.

Our Story

LOOP was born from a simple idea: most people learn skills but struggle to apply them in real projects. We started as a small community of learners, developers, marketers, and creators who wanted to change that.

What began as weekend hackathons and peer mentoring sessions quickly grew into a full-fledged company that combines professional IT solutions, digital marketing services, and social media management — all powered by a passionate community that learns by doing.

Community working together

Our Mission

To empower individuals and businesses by providing high-quality IT & digital services while creating real-world learning opportunities through community collaboration.

Our Vision

A world where learning never stops at theory — where every skill learned is immediately applied, every project builds confidence, and every person has the chance to grow professionally.
